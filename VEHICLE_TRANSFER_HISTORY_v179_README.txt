Nabd Al-Gharb Desktop v1.7.9
Vehicle Transfer and Sent Stock History, Module 2

Completed:
1. Every new vehicle movement receives a clear reference number.
2. Vehicle transfers use VTX references.
3. Warehouse returns use VRT references.
4. Damage records use VDM references.
5. Missing records use VMS references.
6. Existing movement records are upgraded with references automatically.
7. Transfer History now shows reference, vehicle, product/model, quantity, current balance, movement type and user.
8. History can be filtered by sent, returned, damaged or missing movements.
9. Clicking a movement reference opens the complete company document.
10. The movement document uses the company English and Arabic header, VAT, CR, telephone and national address.
11. The document includes source, destination, vehicle, user, product, quantity, price and current vehicle balance.
12. Print and Save PDF are available from the document preview.
13. Vehicle Sales and Settlement Report movement references are clickable too.
14. Cashier access remains limited to assigned vehicle documents.

No new Supabase SQL is required for this module because references are saved inside the existing synced vehicle movement records.
