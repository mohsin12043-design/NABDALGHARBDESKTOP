Nabd Al-Gharb Desktop v1.9.0
Mini Store Settlement Auto Quantity

Added:
1. Product / Model selection in Mini Store Stock Settlement.
2. Remaining Mini Store Quantity loads automatically when a product is selected.
3. Product options show the current remaining balance.
4. Quantity maximum is limited to the available Mini Store stock.
5. The quantity can still be reduced for a partial warehouse return, damage entry or missing entry.
6. Changing the Mini Store or product refreshes the quantity immediately.
7. Existing assigned Mini Store permissions remain active.
8. Existing transfer history and clickable movement documents remain active.

Example:
Transferred to Mini Store: 100 pieces
Sold: 10 pieces
Remaining quantity shown automatically: 90 pieces

Database:
No new Supabase SQL is required for this module.

Build:
The Windows installer was not compiled in this environment. Build it once through GitHub Actions after all Mini Store modules are complete.
