Nabd Al-Gharb Desktop v1.8.1
Vehicle Sale and One Click Vehicle Bill

Completed:
1. One Click Vehicle Bill now uses the same company Tax Invoice print layout.
2. Cash Invoice and Credit Invoice are available.
3. Quotation is not available in the Vehicle Bill.
4. Payment status appears as Paid or Pending.
5. Customer name, VAT, mobile, address and national address fields were added.
6. Vehicle and assigned cashier details appear on the invoice.
7. Product code, description, quantity, unit price, subtotal, VAT 15 percent and grand total appear in the same Tax Invoice structure.
8. ZATCA QR generation is attempted and an offline-safe placeholder is shown when the QR library is unavailable.
9. The existing Tax Invoice vehicle sale flow remains active and deducts sold quantities from the selected vehicle stock.
10. Existing cashier scope, transfer history and settlement auto quantity features remain included.

No new Supabase SQL is required for this version.
Windows installer must be generated with GitHub Actions.
