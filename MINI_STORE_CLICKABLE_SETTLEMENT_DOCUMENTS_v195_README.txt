NABD AL-GHARB DESKTOP v1.9.5 - MINI STORE CLICKABLE SETTLEMENT DOCUMENTS

Added:
1. Mini Store sale invoice numbers are clickable in Sales & Settlement Report.
2. MTX, MRT, MDM, MMS, MCR, MCN and MCRQ references remain clickable.
3. Clicking a sale opens the exact stored invoice in the company Tax Invoice layout.
4. Stored invoice fields are normalized for older and newer sale record formats.
5. Customer, Mini Store, cashier, products, quantity, price, VAT and total are shown.
6. The selected invoice can be closed, printed or saved as PDF.
7. Cashiers can open invoices only for their assigned Mini Store.
8. Opening a report invoice never deducts stock or creates a duplicate sale.
9. Existing Vehicle Stock and previous Mini Store features are preserved.

WINDOWS INSTALLER
