MINI STORE TAX INVOICE LAYOUT

1. One Click Mini Store Bill now uses the same company Tax Invoice layout used by the main billing system.

5. Credit days and due date are included for credit invoices.

10. Amount in words and ZATCA VAT QR are included.
11. Print and Save PDF use the same A4 company layout.
12. Existing finalized customer Tax Invoice sales continue to deduct stock from the selected Mini Store.
13. Existing Mini Store permissions, transfer history and settlement auto quantity remain unchanged.
