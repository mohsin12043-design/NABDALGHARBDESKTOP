Mini Store Cashier Scope - Module 1

Completed:
1. Cashier Mini Store page is limited to assigned Mini Store locations.
2. Cashier cannot see or use Mini Store Setup.
3. Cashier cannot transfer stock from Main Warehouse.
4. Cashier cannot change price visibility or Testing Stock Mode.
5. Cashier can view assigned Mini Store inventory, settlement, bill and own movement history.
6. Movement history is filtered to assigned Mini Stores only.
7. Inventory permission opens My Mini Store Inventory when the cashier is assigned only to a Mini Store.
8. If a cashier is assigned to both Vehicle and Mini Store, Inventory asks which assigned inventory to open.
9. Inventory-only mode hides settlement, reports, bill and history.
10. Admin keeps complete Mini Store and Main Warehouse controls.
11. Hidden admin actions also have code-level permission checks.
12. Cashier settlement product list shows only products available in the assigned Mini Store and does not expose Main Warehouse quantities.

No new Supabase SQL is required for this module.
