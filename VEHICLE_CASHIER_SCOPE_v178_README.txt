Vehicle Cashier Scope - Module 1

Completed:
1. Cashier Vehicle Stock page is limited to assigned vehicle locations.
2. Cashier cannot see or use Vehicle Setup.
3. Cashier cannot transfer stock from Main Warehouse.
4. Cashier cannot change Testing Stock Mode or negative stock settings.
5. Cashier can view assigned vehicle inventory, settlement, vehicle bill and own movement history.
6. Transfer history is filtered to assigned vehicles only.
7. Giving a cashier only Inventory permission opens My Vehicle Inventory instead of Main Warehouse Inventory.
8. Inventory-only mode hides settlement, reports, bill and history.
9. Admin keeps the complete Vehicle Stock and Main Warehouse Inventory screens.
10. Hidden admin actions also have code-level permission checks.
11. Cashier settlement product list shows only products available in the assigned vehicle and does not expose Main Warehouse quantities.
