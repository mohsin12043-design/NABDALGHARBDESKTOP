Nabd Al-Gharb Desktop v1.8.5
Clickable Vehicle Sales and Settlement Documents

Completed:
1. Sales invoice numbers in Vehicle Sales and Settlement Report are now clickable.
2. Clicking a sale invoice opens the complete company Tax Invoice layout.
3. The sale document shows customer, vehicle, cashier, products, quantity, price, VAT and total.
4. Cash and Credit invoice type and payment status are shown when available in the sale record.
5. Transfer, Return, Damage, Missing, Correction, Cancellation and Correction Request references remain clickable.
6. The document title changes automatically according to the selected record.
7. Print and Save PDF use the same company layout already used in the application.
8. Cashiers can open documents only for their assigned vehicle.
9. Admin can open documents for all vehicles.
10. No new Supabase SQL is required.

Data compatibility:
The invoice reader supports existing sale field names including invoice_no, no, items, lines, qty, quantity, price, unit_price, VAT and grand total variations.
