Vehicle Settlement Auto Quantity

Completed Module 3:
1. Vehicle Stock Settlement product list shows current remaining quantity.
2. Selecting a product or model automatically fills the Quantity field with the current vehicle balance.
3. Example: 100 transferred and 10 sold results in 90 automatically loaded.
4. Quantity remains editable for a partial return, damaged stock or missing stock entry.
5. The Quantity field maximum is limited to the current available vehicle stock.
6. Changing the vehicle refreshes the product list and auto quantity.
7. Changing the product refreshes the auto quantity.
8. Existing cashier vehicle restrictions remain active.
9. No new Supabase SQL is required for this module.
