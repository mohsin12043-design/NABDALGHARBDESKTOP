Desktop v2.2.6: customer VAT, address and mobile isolation

Changing the selected customer in Tax Invoice replaces VAT, mobile, address and
national address with that customer's own saved details. Unknown customers start
with empty details. Cash Receipt also clears the previous customer's mobile.

Existing customer profiles are edited in Customer Ledger. Invoice and receipt
history no longer fills or modifies an existing customer's details. An explicit
blank saved in Customer Ledger remains blank when older cloud data is merged.
Each explicit profile edit is saved as a complete version of that customer's
details. Different names sharing a legacy ID are separated instead of merged.

Previously stored wrong details cannot be inferred from the displayed invoice.
Open the affected customer in Customer Ledger, choose Edit, enter the verified
VAT/address (or clear fields that should be empty), and Save once in this version.
No production records or database schema were changed while developing this fix.

The invoice-link review and Copy as New Invoice features from v2.2.5 are preserved.
The only embedded modules changed in v2.2.6 are the customer details paths in
Tax Invoice, Cash Receipt and Customer Ledger, plus their shared merge logic.
