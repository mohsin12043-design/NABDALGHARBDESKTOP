Desktop v2.2.5: invoice customer links

Customer Ledger invoice numbers open a review showing the saved invoice customer,
date and amount. Admin can correct a wrong customer link after reviewing it.
If the invoice already exists under the right customer, the extra wrong link is
removed without adding a second bill. A backup and the original entry are kept.

Invoice history has Open and Copy as New Invoice. Copy loads the products and
customer details with a new number and today's date. It does not post another
bill or change stock until normal PDF/Print saving occurs.

New numbers consider known synced history, existing ledger references and the
device counter. New saved invoices carry an immutable reference and customer ID.
Existing records with a conflicting number are preserved instead of overwritten.
Simultaneous offline creation on older app versions can still create collisions;
those historical cases need review instead of guessing a customer or amount.

For INV-1031, open Nasir Bagali's ledger, click INV-1031, compare the saved Nabd Al
Gharb invoice, and choose Correct customer link. The app asks for confirmation.
Because the screenshots show different dates, this version does not silently
rewrite your live financial data. No production database changes were performed.
